🏦 Business Loan Options

💰 Pradhan Mantri MUDRA Yojana

Provides credit support to eligible micro and small business activities through participating financial institutions.

Categories: Shishu, Kishor and Tarun.

🚀 Visit Official Website

🏭 PMEGP

Prime Minister's Employment Generation Programme supports eligible individuals and organisations in setting up new micro enterprises.

Useful for: Starting eligible new micro-enterprise activities.

🚀 Visit Official Portal

🏢 Stand-Up India

Supports eligible entrepreneurs from SC/ST communities and women entrepreneurs for setting up greenfield enterprises.

🚀 Visit Official Portal

📄 Commonly Required Documents

  • Aadhaar Card
  • PAN Card
  • Address Proof
  • Bank Account Details
  • Business / Project Details
  • Business Plan or Project Report
  • Income / Financial Documents, where applicable
  • Passport-size Photograph

📌 Basic Process

  1. Select a suitable business loan scheme.
  2. Check the scheme and lender eligibility.
  3. Prepare the required documents and business details.
  4. Apply through the official portal or participating lender.
  5. Complete verification and loan processing.
