Get guidance for Learner's Licence (LLR), Driving Licence (DL), required documents and application process.

📋 Application Process

1️⃣ Learner's Licence

Apply for a Learner's Licence and complete the required test and verification process.

2️⃣ Driving Licence

After obtaining the Learner's Licence and completing the required period, apply for the Driving Licence.

3️⃣ Driving Test

Attend the driving test at the concerned RTO according to your appointment.

4️⃣ Licence Issuance

After successful verification and test completion, the Driving Licence is issued.

📄 Commonly Required Documents

🪪 Identity Proof
🏠 Address Proof
🎂 Age / Date of Birth Proof
📸 Photograph
📝 Application Details
🏥 Medical Certificate, where applicable
