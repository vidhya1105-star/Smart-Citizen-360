📄 Commonly Required Documents

  • Proof of Identity
  • Proof of Address
  • Date of Birth Proof
  • Aadhaar Card, where applicable
  • Recent Passport-size Photograph, where applicable
  • Existing Passport for re-issue, if applicable

📌 Basic Application Process

  1. Register or log in to the Passport Seva portal.
  2. Choose the required passport service.
  3. Fill in the application details.
  4. Pay the applicable fee and schedule an appointment.
  5. Visit the Passport Seva Kendra with required documents.
  6. Complete verification and track your application.
